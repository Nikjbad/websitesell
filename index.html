Questions Answered

Frequently Asked
Questions

How long does it take to build a website?

Project timelines depend on complexity. A starter website is typically ready in 5–7 days. Professional projects take 10–14 days. Larger enterprise platforms are scoped individually. We always provide a confirmed timeline before commencing work.

Do you work with clients across India and internationally?

Absolutely. We work with clients across India and internationally. All communication is handled via WhatsApp, email, or video calls, ensuring a seamless experience regardless of your location.

Will my website be mobile-friendly?

Every single website we build is 100% responsive and mobile-first. We test across all major devices — phones, tablets, laptops, and desktops — to guarantee a flawless experience everywhere.

Can I update my website content myself?

Yes. For clients who need content management flexibility, we build on CMS platforms like WordPress so you can easily update text, images, and blog posts without any coding knowledge. Alternatively, we offer a content update service as part of our support plans.

What information do I need to provide to get started?

To begin, we typically need your brand assets (logo if available), preferred colors or reference websites you admire, the services/products you offer, and any specific pages you require. Don't worry if you don't have everything ready — we'll guide you through our discovery process.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. We typically work on a 50% deposit to commence work, with the remaining 50% due on delivery. For larger enterprise projects, we can arrange milestone-based payment schedules. Reach out to discuss what works best for you.
