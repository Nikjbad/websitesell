FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build my website?

Starter sites are typically delivered in 5–7 days. Professional projects take 10–14 days. Enterprise platforms are scoped individually. We always confirm a delivery timeline before starting work.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Absolutely. Every website is built mobile-first and tested on phones, tablets, laptops, and desktops to guarantee a flawless experience on every screen size.

Do you work with clients outside Delhi / across India?

Yes! We work with clients across India and internationally. All communication happens via WhatsApp, email, or video calls — seamless regardless of your location.

Can I update content on my website myself?

Yes. We can build on WordPress CMS for easy self-managed content, or provide a simple admin panel. Alternatively, our support plans include content updates done for you.

What do I need to provide to get started?

Just your business details, preferred style, services/products, and any reference sites you like. If you don't have a logo yet, we can help with that too. We'll guide you through our discovery form.

What are your payment terms?

We work on a 50% upfront and 50% on delivery basis. For larger enterprise projects, milestone-based payments can be arranged. UPI, bank transfer, and all major payment methods accepted.
